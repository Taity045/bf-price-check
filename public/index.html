How we check

  1. We read today's price from the product data on the shop's page.
  2. We rebuild the last twelve months from copies of the page saved by the Internet Archive, about one a week.
  3. We re-check each tracked product about once a day, so its history keeps growing.
  4. If the shop's "was" price is higher than anything we've seen recently, we say so.
  5. Some shops block automated checks, and some pages have no archived copies. When that happens we tell you. We never guess a price.
