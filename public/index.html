    Privacy

    What we keep

    • The product links that people check, and the prices we read. We use them to show price history.
    • Shop requests: the shop name, the product link, and your email if you give it. We use your email only to reply about that shop.
    • Price alerts (when they are on): your email and your price. Each alert email has a stop link. An alert ends after about six months.
    • A scrambled form of your internet address, to stop spam. We do not keep the address itself.

    What stays on your device

    Your saved products, stats and badges stay in your browser. We do not receive this list. To remove it, clear your browser data for this site.

    Other services

    We do not use ads or advertising cookies, and we do not sell your data. The site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare counts page visits without cookies (Cloudflare Web Analytics). When a form has a person check, Cloudflare Turnstile runs it. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

    Where do the prices come from?

    • Today's price comes from the shop's product page.
    • Older prices come from public web archive copies of that page (the Internet Archive), and from prices we save when people check a product here.
    • Many products have few or no archive copies. This is true for most Takealot products. So you will often see "Not enough history".
    • After someone checks a product here, we check its price again about once a day and save it. We do the same for some popular Takealot products. So the history grows each day.
    • Some shops do not let automatic checks read their pages. For those products, we save the price only when someone checks it here.
