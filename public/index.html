How it works

  • We read the price on the store page now (from the page's product data).
  • We rebuild the past 6 months from copies saved by the Internet Archive, about one per week.
  • We aim to re-check each tracked product daily, so its history grows over time.
  • If the "was" price is higher than anything we saw in recent months, we flag it.
  • Some stores block automated checks, and not every product has archived copies. When that happens we tell you. We never guess prices.
Prices can change fast. Always confirm on the store's page before you buy.
