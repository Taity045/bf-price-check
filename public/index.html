          Where do the prices come from?

          • Today's price comes from the shop's product page.
          • Older prices come from public web archive copies of that page, and from prices we save when people check a product here. We use two archives: the Internet Archive, and Common Crawl, a free public archive of the web that saves copies of many shop pages about once a month.
          • We read each archived copy with the same price reader as today's check. We leave out copies that show a bot check, an out-of-stock page, another product, another currency, or a price far away from the product's other prices. Each archived price links to its source.
          • Many products have few or no archive copies, for example most Takealot products, because Takealot does not let archives copy its pages. With 1 or 2 older prices, we show what we found, with the date and the source. From 3 older prices, we give a full verdict.
          • After someone checks a product here, we check its price again about once a day and save it. We do the same for some popular Takealot products. So the history grows each day.
          • Some shops do not let automatic checks read their pages. For those products, we save the price only when someone checks it here.
